The Zulis Configuration located in [[Yrrdstill]] is a collection of 7 statues representing the [[Deities]] the [[Seven Tribes]] worship. The statues are arranged so that if you were to draw a line between each of them you could form an infinity symbol, symbolising the eternity of prosperity one will receive after moving into the afterlife. The configuration is a controversial landmark as those who are directly connected to the [[Seven Tribes]] wish for people to not visit although it has became the number one tourist place for the [[Northern half of Alcheria]]. 

The Zulis Configuration is said to have been built after [[Werlaz]] of the [[Apocian]] tribe brought the other six tribes together to showcase unity between all.
