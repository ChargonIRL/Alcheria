The Seven Tribes were tribes that inhabited all of [[Alcheria]] over 1000 years ago that all different viewpoints of the land and how it should be treat in accordance to each of their different [[Deities]]. However due to the vicious slaughter at the hands of [[King Luran]] and his army during the [[War of the Magic]] the tribes fled north past the [[Molden Mountains]] as [[King Luran]] claimed [[Alcheria]] as his own. The tribes did not get on well with each other in this strange situation they found themselves in as for most of their history they agreed to stay out of each others way, however that would not be the case any longer. [[Werlaz]] the leader of the [[Apocian]] tribe, who were originally from the north eventually brought all the leaders of the tribes together to broker peace between them in these difficult times and together they created the [[Zulis Configuration]].  


The Seven Tribes include:
- [[Apocian]] a tribe of Aarakocras
- [[Harpion]] a tribe of Water Gensai
- [[Zarm]] a tribe of Orcs
- [[Tirmu]] a tribe of Goliaths
- [[Palgen]] a tribe of Dragonborns
- [[Krulax]] a tribe of Goblins
- [[Limen]] a tribe of Tabaxi
