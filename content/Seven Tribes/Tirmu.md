The Tirmu tribe were a group of goliaths who followed the teaching of the God of War [[Zorath]]. The title "God of War" is thought to be a mistranslation however, with it more likely being the God of Protection. Because of the mistranslation many believe that Tirmu goliaths were conquering beasts who would attack all that they saw, but it was more likely that they kept to themselves not mingling with others focusing on their sole protection. They originated in modern day [[Lazil]] in the [[Molden Mountains]] most likely using the mountains as an easy way to protect half of their tribe. During the [[War of the Magic]] they did not want to leave their home believing that they could protect against the invading Alcherian army on their own. They did however agree to create the [[Zulis Configuration]] as a symbol of unity between the other tribes. Near the end of the war the Alcherian army attacked the Tirmu tribe and overwhelmed them quite easily but were unable to get fully across the mountains allowing the survival of the other tribes. The Tirmu tribe are seen as a cautionary tale to the other tribes on why unity is necessary between them.

The Tirmu tribe is represented by the colour black and their symbol is a wall
