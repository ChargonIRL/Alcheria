The Krulax tribe are a group of goblins who are some of the kindest in all of [[Alcheria]]. They follow in the footsteps of [[Micil]] the God of Abundance. Krulax goblins are known for giving away a lot of food and other essentials to those in need or even to those passing by. If you meet a Krulax goblin there is no stopping them from inviting you for a hearty meal, either cooked by them or paid for by them. They are the friendliest of the [[Seven Tribes]] towards outsiders and mainly live off the lands resources that will always be plentiful due to their worship of [[Micil]]. They were originally from modern day [[Progenis]].


The Krulax tribe is represented by the colour yellow and their symbol is a coin
