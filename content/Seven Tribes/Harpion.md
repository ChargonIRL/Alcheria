The Harpion Tribe is composed of completely water genasi who all worship their Goddess [[Tapol]] who is the Goddess of the Sea and water in general. They were originally from near where modern [[Ingaran]] is situated and had the hardest time adjusting to moving north because of the [[War of the Magic]]. Because they believe that [[Tapol]] is all forms of water in the world they can be seen by some as quite arrogant thinking that they are offshoots of the Goddess themselves. They are kind to most as most have water within them in some form however they can be quite abrasive with pushing their beliefs onto others.


The Harpion tribe is represented by the colour blue and its symbol is the water drop.
