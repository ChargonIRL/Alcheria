The Limen tribe are a group of tabaxi who worship the God of the Land [[Nirbul]]. They were originally located in modern day [[Crimsworth]] as in the past it was a lush forest that they were able to roam through alongside [[Nirbul]]. Unfortunately during the [[Alcherian War]] the forest was burned to the ground forcing both the tribe and [[Nirbul]] to flee north. Due to this they are the most wary of outsiders as they have been hurt before. They are also quite spiritual and focus on protecting the land from those wishing to exploit it, a lot of the tribe are druidic in nature although it is not something forced upon them. They have a general distaste of technology especially coming from [[Crimsworth]].


The Limen tribe is represented by the colour green and their symbol is a paw print
