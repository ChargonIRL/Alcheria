The Apocian tribe are a group of Aarakocras who were originally located near the modern city of [[Yrrdstill]]. The Apocian tribe mainly worship [[Baroe]] the God of the Sky as being Aarakocras they are quite connected with the sky. They believe that the light of the sun is actually [[Baroe]] travelling the world. The Apocian tribe and their leader [[Werlaz]] were the tribe who brought all the other tribes together and welcomed them into their land after the [[Alcherian War]]. Apocians are very welcoming to outsiders as long as they pose no threat to them or the other tribes.

The Apocian tribe is represented by the colour white and their symbol is a feather.
