Micil is the God of Abundance in the pantheon of the [[Seven Tribes]] and was originally worshipped by the [[Krulax]] tribe and is now worshipped by a wide variety of people including some in the south. Unfortunately those in the south see Micil as a way to give abundance to oneself however those in the north and the [[Krulax]] tribe understand that Micil gives abundance to communities in struggle rather than individuals mostly. Micil is said to be a large portly goblin who is constantly seen surrounded by food and gold, he is said to be quite generous with these allowing people to take some if they are lucky enough to encounter him. In [[Krulax]] texts he apparently travels the world seeking out those in need of him most.
