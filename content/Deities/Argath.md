Argath is the official deity of the [[Alcheria]] Kingdom and is said to be a man with flowing blond hair clad in pristine white armour symbolising the strength he gave to all Alcherian citizens. He is usually depicted wielding the [[Mosath Shield]], a red tower shield that can deflect all evil and it is said he uses it to protect all of [[Alcheria]]. He is said to have created all of [[Locath]] and all who live on it. He is seen by a benevolent God by most though some see him as a symbol of the [[Alcherian War]].

Argath is represented by the lambda symbol (**λ**). He is also known to be connected with lambs, eyes and the yarrow plant.
