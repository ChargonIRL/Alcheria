Nirbul is the God of the Land in the pantheon of the [[Seven Tribes]] and is mainly worshipped by the [[Limen]] tribe but is also minorly worshipped by the other tribes. This is because it is said that Nirbul is the creator of the land and the animals that roam it including all the intelligent species, he was able to do this with the help of [[Tapol]] who provided her water to allow life to grow. He is seen as an honorary second in command behind [[Cyrs]] as even though he created the world he does not do much in protecting it allowing his creations to do as they please even if it were to hurt other creations of his. Nirbul is described to have the head of a deer, the body of a bear and tail of a lion and is said to reside in the [[Oolo Forest]] at the north most point of Alcheria. However he did not always roam that forest as during the [[Alcherian War]] his original forest was destroyed by the Alcherian soldiers and so he moved north along with the other tribes who were pushed out of their home.
