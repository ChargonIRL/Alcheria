Cyrs is the Goddess of Love in the pantheon of the [[Seven Tribes]] and is worshipped by almost all in the north in some way but is especially worshipped by the [[Zarm]] tribe. Cyrs is said to look different to every person, in text they mainly appear as a woman all though there have been certain texts saying people have saw him as a man and it is widely accepted. Cyrs does not create love in the sense that two strangers would wildly get in love with each other there has to be something there and she mainly amplifies feelings of all types of love from platonic to romantic. As mentioned anyone in the north who worships any God in the pantheon will most likely also worship Cyrs as they are said to have brought all the [[Deities]] together and prevent them from infighting even though the [[Zarm]] tribe was not the one to bring all the tribes together. Because of this he is seen as the honorary head of the pantheon even if all the Gods are on the same level.
