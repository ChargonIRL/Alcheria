Tapol is the Goddess of the Sea in the pantheon of the [[Seven Tribes]] and is mainly worshipped by the [[Harpion]] Tribe. Tapol is described as being all water in the world from lakes and rivers, to the seas and oceans and even the water in people. However she has been known to take form in some texts in which she is described as looking like a large head with long flowing hair of waves that crash at the end. The [[Harpion]] tribe being mainly water genasi obviously have a very close connection to Tapol as it is said each are created by Tapol to bring life to the world. This means that the [[Harpion]] tribe are the most spiritual of the tribes as they have this deep connection with their Goddess. Tapol is said to have also helped [[Nirbul]] in the creation of the land and animals as her water was the key piece that was missing allowing the world to flourish as it does now.
