Zorath was the God of War for the pantheon of the [[Seven Tribes]] and was worshipped by the [[Tirmu]] tribe. The title of God of War can be linked to a mistranslation as Zorath was more about protecting oneself and their community rather than conquering others. Unfortunately that mistranslation permutates to today as cults have formed worshipping Zorath believing he will destroy those who don't kneel before him. 

The [[Tirmu]] were quite solitary and were opposed to the unification of the [[Seven Tribes]] as it would be harder to keep their individuality when combined with all of the tribes. However despite this they eventually did join together to create the [[Zulis Configuration]]. Unfortunately they kept quite solitary and therefore did not have the numbers to defeat the Alcherian army near the end of the [[Alcherian War]]. Most text was destroyed in the battle so not much is known about the true form of Zorath.

In the few remaining [[Tirmu]] texts Zorath is described as being the reason why the [[Molden Mountains]] were formed as he pulled the earth beneath him upwards to protect the [[Tirmu]] from dangers in the north. 

He as well as his tribe have now became a cautionary tale for the rest of the [[Seven Tribes]] of why they must always stick together and be unified.
