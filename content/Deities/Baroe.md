Baroe is the God of the sky in the pantheon of the [[Seven Tribes]] and is mainly worshipped by the [[Apocian]] tribe although others in the north worship him as well. Baroe is described as an eagle the size of a mountain with feathers of pearlescent white that radiate light to land below. In [[Apocian]] folklore Baroe was the explanation given to why the sun moves across the sky as they believed that Baroe was the sun itself as he travelled across the land spreading his light. Baroe is seen as a benevolent god providing the light of the world but also the winds as he flaps his wings to travel across the sky.
