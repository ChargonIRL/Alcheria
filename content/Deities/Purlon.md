Purlon is the Goddess of Death in the pantheon of the [[Seven Tribes]] and is almost exclusively followed by those of the [[Palgen]] tribe due to a lack of knowledge about Purlon. She is described as an ethereal entity with a large quantity of heads of differing species that constantly shift and change over time. Her body is described as looking like "all that has been before and all that will come" in old [[Palgen]] texts. Purlon is seen by the tribe as a constant in all lives ever looming but instead of a feeling of dread over that fact the [[Palgen]] tribe feel warmth over the fact that one day Purlon will take them to all they have lost throughout there lives. In this way Purlon is not a deity to be afraid of as many assume but as a being that will eventually visit you and will return what has been lost.
