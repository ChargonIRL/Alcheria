The Verzan Monastery is located in a remote region in the south west of [[Alcheria]], the nearest large city is [[Turzil]] though it is quite a distance. The head of the monastery is Saint Cermus (Pronounced: Sir-mus), a half elf with long blond hair who wears long white robes and a pendant of a lambda (**λ**), the symbol of [[Argath]]. The monastery although remote does house a large number of prospective monks following in the steps of [[Argath]]. 

There are six other similar monasteries dotted around Alcheria each headed by their own saint and each following a similar hierarchy being:
Top - Saints
2nd - Lanpers
3rd - Prolecs
Bottom - Monks
These titles apart from Saints are purely ceremonial to those outside of the monasteries though the titles do carry weight to those within the hierarchy.

Saint Cermus is said to have seen [[Argath]] when he was young and [[Argath]] bestowed upon him many teachings and gave him a fraction of his power. Cermus is a kind man to the monks under him and talks at length about the power of [[Argath]] and his encounter. Being the head of the monastery and a saint he has a good relation with [[King Acaron]] who is said to have been chosen by [[Argath]] to be the king of [[Alcheria]].  

Every year on the anniversary of the end of the [[War of the Magic]] he selects 5 monks to travel with him to [[Progenis]] to be apart of the parade and officially promote then to Prolecs.
