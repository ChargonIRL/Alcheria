Pronounced: Mia-zin

Miaxin is a city on the border between [[Alcheria]] and [[Griweth]] situated in the east of [[Alcheria]]. Miaxin is seen as quite a rough place for the [[Southern half of Alcheria]] and therefore is an inexpensive place to live. This is due to the recent issues between [[Alcheria]] and [[Griweth]] that have upset the relationship that had been in place for quite a while. The issues stem to how the King and Queen treat the [[Seven Tribes]] and more specifically the [[Zarm]] tribe as Miaxin was their home for quite some time before the [[War of the Magic]]. [[Griweth]] was quite upset over the war but were not in a place to be able to defend Miaxin from the Alcherian army and so struck a deal to have a friendly relationship with the new country. Unfortunately over the past 10 years [[Alcheria]] are slacking in their end of the deal which was to help maintain [[Miaxin]] as a symbol of good faith with the [[Zarm]] tribe.
