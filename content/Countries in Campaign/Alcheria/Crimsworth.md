Crimsworth is an industrial town situated to the south east of [[Alcheria]] and previously was a forest that the [[Limen]] tribe used to roam through with their deity [[Nirbul]]. Unfortunately during the [[Alcherian War]] the Alcherian army burned the  forest to the ground and forced both the [[Limen]] tribe and [[Nirbul]] to flee north past the [[Molden Mountains]]. [[Alcheria]] then built Crimsworth up to become a titan of industry. The city is quite inexpensive due to the smog permutating the streets and so most living there are in the depths of poverty working at the factories just trying to survive. Although most are quite poor there are a few brilliant inventors in Crimsworth who live there due to easy availability to materials.
