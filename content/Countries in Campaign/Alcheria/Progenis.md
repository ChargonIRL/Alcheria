Progenis is the capital city of [[Alcheria]] and is where both [[King Acaron]] and [[Queen Namoca]] currently reside. It is situated in the west, south of the [[Molden Mountains]] There is a wide range of people living in Progenis from those at the very top of the financial hierarchy to people barely scratching by. Progenis is the ancestral homeland of the [[Krulax]] tribe which they had to flee from during the [[War of the Magic]]. Progenis itself like its people is a real mixed bag. The closer to the castle you get the more grand and expensive the area becomes but on the far outstretches the city can be seen as quite run down with some unsavoury activity happening.
