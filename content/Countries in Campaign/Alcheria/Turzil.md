Turzil is the second biggest city in all of [[Alcheria]] and is also the location of the [[Perzath Academy]], the most prestigious magic academy in the country. It is located in the south west of [[Alcheria]]. There is a potent magical aura surrounding the city that means that students at the academy have an easier time learning the magic craft compared to people in other parts of the country. The magical aura is said to be there due to the [[Palgen]] tribe who originally lived in the area before the [[War of the Magic]] and their connection to the Goddess of Death [[Purlon]]. Also due to the magical aura and the prestige of the [[Perzath Academy]] Turzil is a mighty expensive place to live and study with the majority of students being children of alumni. People from Turzil are seen to be quite arrogant as they believe they are more in tune with the magical energies of the world compared to the common person.
