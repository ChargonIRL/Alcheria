Pronounced: Yerd-still

Yrrdstill is the biggest city in the north of [[Alcheria]] and can be argued to be the only "city" in the north to begin with. Yrrdstill is homeland of the [[Apocian]] tribe and most still live there to this day but the city is very multicultural with a lot from the [[Seven Tribes]] deciding to live there. It is situated near the [[Zulis Configuration]] and seen as inexpensive to live in compared to [[Alcheria]] as a whole but more expensive than the rest of the north. Yrrdstill is said to be where most of the [[Seven Tribes]] fled to during the [[Alcherian War]] and was built up as an epicentre for the remaining tribes. People from Yrrdstill are quite friendly towards most people although can be cold to some southerners.
