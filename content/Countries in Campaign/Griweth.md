Griweth is a nation that mainly borders the [[Northern half of Alcheria]] to its north east, but has a slight border with the southern half mainly near [[Miaxin]]. Griweth has a turbulent relationship with [[Alcheria]] due to them attacking the [[Zarm]] tribe during the [[Alcherian War]]. They are friendlier with those in the north and tensions seem to be cooling in recent times. It is ruled in a democracy.
